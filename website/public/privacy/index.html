Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

MayhemStats (mayhemstats.com) is a community fan project showing aggregate statistics for the ARAM Mayhem game mode. Privacy is the project's founding constraint: the system was designed so that player identity is never collected in the first place, rather than collected and protected. This page describes exactly what data exists and what doesn't.

Game data contributed through MayhemStats Tracker

The MayhemStats Tracker desktop app records your matches locally, on your machine. If you enable the optional community contribution setting (off by default), the app uploads an anonymous record of each match containing:

  • For each of the ten players: champion, augments, items, and combat statistics (kills, deaths, assists, damage, healing, gold, multikills)
  • Match metadata: queue, game patch, duration, creation time, and region plus game id (used only to count a match once when several players contribute it)

Never uploaded: summoner names, Riot IDs, tag lines, profile icons, PUUIDs, or any other player identifier — for you or the other nine players in your games. The community database contains no identity columns at all.

Uploads are associated with a random token generated on your machine, derived from nothing, used only to rate-limit abuse and to honor deletion requests. Using Settings → Community Stats → Delete my contributions in the app removes every record your install has shared; matches no other player contributed are deleted entirely.

Data collected by this website

  • Hosting logs: the site is served by Vercel, whose infrastructure processes IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and protect the service, as any web host does.
  • Local caching: the site stores public game metadata (champion, augment, and item names) in your browser's local storage to speed up repeat visits. This contains no personal data and never leaves your browser.
  • Analytics: the site currently runs no analytics and sets no tracking cookies of its own.
  • Third-party content: champion, augment, and item images and names load in your browser from Riot's Data Dragon and the community-run CommunityDragon CDN; those requests are made directly by your browser to those services.

Advertising

MayhemStats may display advertisements served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense to cover hosting costs. Ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including, where permitted, personalized ads; where required by law you will be asked for consent before any such cookies are set, and you can decline. Google's use of advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Advertising applies to this website only — game data contributions are never used for advertising and contain no identity to advertise to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. The community game database holds no personal data to access or correct; deletion of contributed match records is self-service through the app as described above. For anything else, contact the maintainer by opening an issue at the project's GitHub repository.

Changes to this policy

If the site's data practices change — for example, when advertising or analytics are introduced — this page will be updated and the date above revised before those changes take effect.
